The 4 Stages of a Manufactured Firing

Toxic managers don't fire high performers out of nowhere—they build a gradual trap to avoid paying severance and unemployment. Here is how they operate:

01

The Quiet Isolation

You are excluded from strategy syncs, left off email chains, and your Slack messages sit unacknowledged. The goal: create self-doubt.

02

The Shifting Goalposts

Deadlines are moved up verbally behind closed doors. Scope is doubled with no extra resources so you fail organically on paper.

03

The PIP Ambush

You are handed a 30-day "Performance Improvement Plan" full of vague subjective buzzwords ("lack of culture alignment") to justify termination.

04

The Zero-$ Pushout

Daily micro-humiliations designed to make you quit voluntarily so they pay $0 severance and block your state unemployment benefits.

The Confirmatory Memo That Stops Them Cold

When a manager gives a dangerous verbal order or yells at you behind closed doors, send this email within 90 minutes with your private email BCC'd:

SUBJECT: Confirmation of discussion regarding [Project] timeline - [Date] BCC: personal@gmail.com

Hi [Manager Name],

Thank you for meeting with me at [Time] today to discuss the [Project] deliverables.

To ensure complete alignment and that our team remains on track, I am writing to confirm our conversation and agreed next steps:

  1. You indicated that the original deadline of [Original Date] has now been moved up to [Accelerated Date].
  2. You noted that [Specific directive, e.g., "the secondary QA review can be bypassed in order to hit this deadline"].
  3. I shared that expediting the schedule carries a measurable risk of [Specific business risk], but that I will proceed strictly according to your directive.

Please reply if any of the points above do not reflect our agreed direction. If I don't hear otherwise, I will proceed on this basis.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

💡 Why this works: It traps the manager. If they agree in writing, they own the failure. If they don't reply, your contemporaneous record stands legally uncontested.
